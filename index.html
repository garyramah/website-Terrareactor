The Market Opportunity

Reuse the grid connection, land, and water — even when the reactor stays off.

Building gigawatt-scale AI factories from scratch faces severe interconnection delays and permitting bottlenecks. Former nuclear stations, retired coal plants, and legacy industrial sites already possess high-voltage switchyards, industrial water rights, heavy transport access, and established industrial footprints.

Site reuse vs. generation restart — the distinction that unlocks the asset class

As demonstrated by recent European evaluations of shut-down nuclear facilities — in October 2026, PreussenElektra and NVIDIA announced an open-ended exploration of converting former German nuclear sites into AI data centers — the opportunity is not limited to restarting nuclear reactors. Reusing existing grid interconnections, substations, and cooling infrastructure unlocks a global inventory of retired thermal, nuclear, and industrial assets for compute redevelopment. The reactors are not restarted. The site is reused.

PILLAR 01

Electrical switchyards & grid ties

Existing 220kV–380kV+ transmission corridors and substation footprints — where actual import capacity must be separated from physical line presence.

PILLAR 02

Cooling & water infrastructure

Legacy intake/discharge structures, river or coastal proximity, and historical water withdrawal permits that require updated thermal and regulatory review.

PILLAR 03

Industrial land & buffer zones

Pre-zoned heavy industrial acreage with structural load capacity, security perimeters, and rail/heavy-haul access.

PILLAR 04

Decommissioning & environmental timeline

Clear tracking of dismantling milestones, parcel release schedules, and soil/radiological or remediation boundaries.

Architecture // Powered by Agentics

Autonomous document discovery. Mandatory human verification.
